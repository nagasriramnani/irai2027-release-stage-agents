## reviewer

**summary:** I reviewed this as a strict IEEE programme-committee reviewer for a responsible-AI conference, using the ieee-reviewer skill (conference venue, full manuscript, AI/ML domain, harsh). I read paper/main.tex, both macro files, PROTOCOL.md v1.3a, pilot/analysis/REPORT.md, the earlier audit summary and the compiled PDF (6 pages; page 6 right column ends at about 60% height). Nothing was edited and Ollama was not called.

**Contribution and novelty.** This is a controlled, fully synthetic factorial study: five simulated release packages crossed with prompt-only versus tool-level permission enforcement. It has trace-based measures (SAR, SuAR, ABCR) and a fault-adjusted estimand defined by a scripted reference agent. Compared with LayerRAG-Bench (stale and denied faults) and "Prompts Don't Protect" (prompt versus architectural enforcement), what is new is the combination and the estimand. The measured agent behaviours are:
- The agent follows search order between two versions that are both labelled current.
- It does not retry after a connector fault.
- Most clearly, it ignores source-ID deny lists that contradict the visible label (24/24 runs; Llama 9/12).

**The rejection risk.** Three of the four headline results in the abstract and conclusion follow from the design:
- The development and integration gaps are mostly the faults the authors injected.
- Tool-level enforcement protects only covered sources because it executes the lagging labels.
- The accuracy-only gate misses refusal failures because accuracy is defined on answerable questions only.

The body already concedes each point (l.65, 104, 151, 157), so the abstract (l.40) and conclusion (l.162) overclaim against the paper's own text. Reframing them around the measured behaviours is the most important fix, and it costs no space.

**Soundness.**
- **Statistics:** the percentile item-cluster bootstrap is anti-conservative when only a few scenarios carry an effect. H2b rests on 5 scenarios and Llama H2c on 5, so an exact sign-flip test cannot go below p = .0625. Yet the paper prints Holm p = .049 and an unadjusted .008 ("replicated").
- **Power:** by the protocol's own §9 rule, pilot-variance power (.20–.88 for H1, .07 for H2c) means scenarios should be added. That decision is still pending and should be made before the confirmatory run.
- **Reference agent:** it picks documents by oracle, so its ABCR is 1 by construction. The Fig. 1 caption overstates what its line shows, and the legend says "Observable ideal" instead of "reference agent".
- **"Preregistered":** the claim has no registry link, while the protocol is an unsigned draft that was revised after pilot results.
- **Scorer validation:** the human audit is only described in the future tense.

**Pilot vs confirmatory framing.** The development split was also used to tune the harness and revise the protocol, so pilot inference is exploratory and should be labelled that way. Discarded pilot iterations should be disclosed in the Data Availability statement. Some pilot numbers sit in black text (l.101, l.157) and will escape the `\pilot{}` sweep.

**Clarity.** Recurring "fell by −0.18" double negatives. "Of which" mixes estimates from different item sets. The reference-agent argument quotes accuracy where SAR is meant. Contribution 1 says five task classes; Section III-A says four. H2c and H4 are never defined.

**Reproducibility.** The paper gives no serving software, quantization, weight digest or context length; run records log only the tag.

**Ethics and AI-use disclosure.** The disclosure meets IEEE's identification requirement. It should name the model version, state that the authors checked every citation, and say that no LLM scores any run.

**Page budget.** The proposed cuts more than pay for the additions:
- Title shortened from 4 lines to 2.
- Abstract trimmed from 266 words to under 250 by dropping the ablation clause.
- The unreported GEE/mixed-model sentence removed, which also frees a reference.
- The unreported "channels" clause removed (l.130).
- The redundant last sentence of the acknowledgment removed.

**Likely decision posture.** If submitted with pilot results: reject (results are placeholders and the significance claims are fragile). With confirmatory data, the abstract and conclusion reframed, the exact-test check added, the registration cited and the power decision resolved: borderline to weak accept as a careful evaluation-methodology paper.
