# IRAI 2027 submission requirements (checked 30 Oct 2026)

Source: <https://irai2027.ieee-ies.org/for_authors/> (fetched 30 Oct 2026). Re-check before paper freeze and before upload.
Venue findings marked † come from the literature workflow (`literature/search_log.md`) and should be re-verified.

## Format
| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Template | IEEE two-column conference format only (IEEEtran `[conference]`) — <https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html> |
| Length | Regular paper ≤ 6 pages **including** title, authors, figures and references (WIP papers ≤ 4) |
| File | PDF ≤ 2 MB; final version through IEEE PDF eXpress (Conference ID not yet announced) |
| Review | **Single-blind**: author names and affiliations stay in the manuscript; 3 reviewers |
| Screening | Plagiarism and duplicate-publication checks |
| System | IEEE IES ConfComm (register: <https://confcomm.ieee-ies.org/authentication/register>); not yet open |

## Policies that affect the manuscript
* **AI-generated content (IEEE policy)†:** disclose use of AI tools for text, figures, or code in the *Acknowledgments*, naming
  the system, the sections, and the level of use. This is separate from describing the agent under test in Methods.
* **Preprints (IEEE policy)†:** arXiv/TechRxiv posting is allowed. The preprint must carry the IEEE "submitted for possible
  publication" notice; never post the final published PDF.
* **PDF eXpress†:** embed all fonts; no hyperlinks or bookmarks in the camera-ready (load `hyperref` with `draft` or drop it).
* **Track fit (checked 31 Oct 2026 on <https://irai2027.ieee-ies.org/program/technical_tracks/index.html>):** the technical
  tracks are TT01–TT08. Submit to **TT04 AI Safety and Security** (secure deployment, AI risk management and assurance,
  privacy-preserving AI). Second choice: **TT06 AI Ethics and Society** (governance and regulatory compliance, auditability).
  "AI Agents and Bots" is in the topic list but has no technical track. No special sessions are listed yet (proposals due
  31 Mar 2027); re-check in April 2027 for one on agent safety or AI assurance. IRAI 2026 (first edition, Melbourne) is the
  only prior edition; accepted papers are submitted to IEEE Xplore. No acceptance rate is published. Every accepted paper must
  be presented (oral 10+5 min or A0 poster; online presentation allowed with a pre-recorded video).
